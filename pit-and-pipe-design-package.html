What's in an NBN pit and pipe design package

What you receive from us, what you upload to NBN, what your contractor builds from, and what is not included.

Reviewed 14 October 2026

In short: you receive a pit and pipe design drawing as a PDF ready to upload to the nbn New Developments portal, plus the matching AutoCAD (DWG) file, and the as-built design after construction. It shows every pit, conduit, road crossing and service drop, with lot numbers and a premises count that match your application. Each issue carries a revision letter and date, one client revision is included, and we action NBN's review comments at no extra charge.

The drawing

The core of the package is a scaled plan of your development showing NBN's network as it will be built:

  • Every new pit, with its NBN pit type and location relative to lot boundaries and kerbs
  • Distribution and local network conduit routes along each street, with sizes (P100, P50) and lengths
  • Road crossings and lateral conduits
  • A P20 service drop to each SDU lot, or the lead-in to each MDU building or common property
  • The connection point to NBN's existing network, or to an earlier stage of your development
  • Existing roads, poles, transformers and telecommunications pits that affect the layout
  • Lot numbers, street names and the total premises count
  • A legend, north point, scale, title block, revision letter and date

File formats

Every design is issued as a PDF and an AutoCAD DWG. The PDF is the file NBN reviews for small developments of up to 20 premises. For larger developments and for MDU pathway designs, NBN strongly advises using its AutoCAD drafting tool, and we prepare the DWG to suit. Your civil contractor can drop the DWG straight into their own drawings.

As-builts

As-built designs are included. Once your contractor has built the works, send us their records of any changes from the design, such as moved pits or different conduit lengths. We update the design to match what was built, ready for you to upload with the Notice of Practical Completion.

Revisions

Each issue of the design carries a revision letter: A for the first issue, then B, C and so on. One revision requested by you is included in the price, for example after your engineer moves a driveway. If NBN raises comments on the design during its review, we action them at no extra charge, unless they come from changes to your development.

What you do with it

  1. Upload the PDF, and DWG if required, to the nbn New Developments portal under your developer account.
  2. Send any NBN comments to us. We revise and reissue.
  3. Once NBN accepts the design, give it to your civil or telecommunications contractor to build.
  4. After construction, send us your contractor's records of what was built. We prepare the as-built design for you to submit with the Notice of Practical Completion.

The NBN process guide explains each stage.

What is not included

  • NBN's fees and charges
  • Supply or installation of pits, conduit, cables or fibre
  • Construction, site works and surveying
  • Electrical design, and design of gas, water or other services

Optional add-ons

Add an NBN application concierge, where we lodge and track the application in the portal for you, or rush turnaround within 5 business days. See add-on services.

Turnaround and price

Designs are turned around within two weeks of receiving complete CAD plans. Pricing is per unit, from $120, with lower rates as the project grows. See pricing or get a quote.

Design package questions

Do I get the CAD file?

Yes. Every design is issued as both a PDF and an AutoCAD DWG file.

How many revisions are included?

One revision requested by you is included. Comments raised by NBN on the design are actioned at no extra charge, unless they result from changes to your development.

Are as-built drawings included?

Yes. After construction we prepare the as-built design from your contractor's records, ready to submit with the Notice of Practical Completion.

Who uploads the design to NBN?

The design is uploaded under your developer account in the nbn New Developments portal. We supply the files ready to upload.
