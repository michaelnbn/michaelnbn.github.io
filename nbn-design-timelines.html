How long does NBN pit and pipe design take?

What our two-week turnaround covers, what can stretch it, and the NBN stages that follow the design.

Reviewed 14 October 2026

In short: NBN Design turns plans around within two weeks of receiving complete CAD plans. After that, NBN reviews the design, your contractor builds it, NBN reviews the as-builts and inspects, and NBN then installs its network. NBN sets its own timeframes for those steps, so plan the design early, ideally as soon as your lot layout is settled.

Our part: two weeks

The clock starts when we have complete CAD plans and the premises count. Design time itself depends mostly on size: a 10 lot subdivision is around two hours of design work, and a 100 lot stage is around two days. The rest of the two weeks covers checking, questions back to you, and fitting each job into the queue.

What can stretch it

  • Incomplete plans. Missing kerbs, driveways, lot numbers or existing services. See preparing CAD plans.
  • Poor quality CAD. Plans that are not to scale, or PDFs that have to be traced.
  • Difficult sites. Transformers, retaining walls, laneways or tight frontages that limit where pits can go.
  • Changes after we start. A new lot layout or driveway plan means part of the design is redone.

If any of these apply, we tell you when we receive the plans, not at the end.

After the design: NBN's stages

  1. 1

    Design

    Within two weeks of complete CAD plans.

  2. 2

    NBN design review

    NBN reviews the uploaded design and accepts it or returns comments. Comments on our design are actioned at no extra charge.

  3. 3

    Construction

    Your contractor builds the pits and conduits, usually alongside the other civil works.

  4. 4

    As-builts and practical completion

    As-builts and the Notice of Practical Completion are uploaded. NBN reviews them and inspects the site.

  5. 5

    Certificate and network install

    NBN issues the Certificate of Practical Completion, installs its network and confirms when the site is in service.

NBN's timeframes for review, inspection and network installation are set by NBN and can vary. Check them with your NBN contact for your development.

How to keep the whole job moving

  • Start the design as soon as the lot layout is stable, before civil works are tendered.
  • Give us driveway and transformer positions up front.
  • Keep the premises count in the design and in your stage application the same.
  • Make sure your contractor builds to the accepted design, with draw cords, end caps and labelled service drops, so the as-builts pass first time.
  • Pre-install the connection box at each home, ideally at frame stage, as NBN recommends.

Why trained designers matter

NBN runs free training for new developments designers and installers. Designers who reach NBN's highest training level, Trained Premium, get instant design acceptance without NBN's pre-acceptance review. Whatever designer you use, a design that is right first time is the biggest saving in the whole process.
