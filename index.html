What we design

Single dwelling units (SDU)

Estates, subdivisions and groups of stand-alone lots, including duplexes and triplexes. NBN requires a dedicated lead-in conduit from a pit into each premises, and we design the pits, routes and conduit sizes.

SDU pit and pipe design

Multi dwelling units (MDU)

Buildings and sites with multiple dwellings in one lot, including apartment buildings and sites where shared land will be owned by a body corporate.

MDU pit and pipe design

Why use an NBN trained designer

NBN recommends working with a trained designer so your development is built right the first time and certification moves faster.

See where design fits in the NBN process

  • Designed to NBN's standard

    Pit locations, conduit sizes and routes, and a lead-in conduit to every premises, all prepared to NBN's current pit and conduit standard.

  • Built right the first time

    NBN's training is built around designs that pass review and are installed correctly, which keeps rework off your programme.

  • Two-week turnaround

    Plans turned around within two weeks of receiving complete CAD plans.

  • Australia-wide

    NBN's new developments process is national. Send plans for a development in any state or territory.
