NBN pit and pipe design: common questions

Short answers about pit and pipe design, SDU and MDU developments, quotes and pricing.

Reviewed 4 October 2026 against NBN's published guidance

The basics

What is pit and pipe design?

It is the design of the underground pits and conduits that carry NBN fibre to each premises in a new development. NBN reviews the design before construction starts.

Who needs a pit and pipe design?

Developers of new developments that NBN will service through pits and conduit. NBN lists multiple stand-alone dwellings, adjoined dwellings, horizontal MDUs, super lots and empty lots on newly built roads as examples.

Do I have to use a trained designer?

The developer is responsible for the design and can arrange it themselves. NBN encourages using an industry professional or an NBN trained supplier so the design is built right the first time.

What is the difference between an SDU and an MDU?

An SDU is a single dwelling unit, such as a stand-alone lot, duplex or triplex, and each premises gets its own P20 service drop conduit. An MDU is a building containing multiple dwellings within a single lot. See SDU design and MDU design.

Working with us

Do you work outside NSW?

Yes. NBN's new developments process is national, and we take projects in every state and territory.

What do you need to give a quote?

The site address, development type, number of lots or dwellings, and your NBN application reference if you have one. CAD (DWG) and/or PDF plans are needed before work starts.

Do I need an NBN application first?

No. You can ask for a quote before an application exists. Tell us the reference if you have one.

What file formats can I send?

CAD (DWG) and PDF. Send a share link in the quote form, or we will send an upload link when we reply.
