NBN pit and pipe design for contractors

For electrical, civil and pit and pipe contractors who build the NBN works but don't keep a designer busy all year. Send us the job and we prepare the design, lodge it with NBN and see it through to close-out.

Reviewed 8 November 2026

In short: we are an overflow NBN designer for contractors. You send the CAD plans, we send a fixed price the same or next business day from our published per-unit rates, design to NBN's current standard within two weeks, lodge the NBN application for you and action NBN's comments. As-builts are included. No minimum fee and no deposit.

Who this is for

  • Electrical contractors and Accredited Service Providers who pick up the NBN scope on a subdivision or townhouse site
  • Pit and pipe and civil contractors who need an approved design before they can build
  • Designers and consultancies with more NBN work than hours

How it works

  1. Send the CAD plans (DWG) and the site address through the quote form.
  2. Get a fixed price the same or next business day, worked out from the published rates.
  3. We design the pits, conduits and lead-ins to NBN's current standard, within two weeks of complete CAD plans.
  4. We lodge the NBN application for you and are added to it as the designer, so you don't have to manage the portal.
  5. NBN's comments are actioned at no extra charge, and as-built drawings are included.

What it costs

The same published rates as every client: $120 per unit for units 1 to 10, then lower bands down to $70 per unit above 100. A 2 lot subdivision is $240 and a 16 townhouse site is $1,848. No minimum fee and no deposit. Use the price calculator for your job.

Invoicing that suits you

Invoice per job, or batch several jobs onto one monthly invoice, whichever is easier for your accounts team.

Why contractors use us

  • Two-week turnaround from complete CAD plans
  • Clear drawings your crew can build from, on our title block, with your logo added if you want it
  • NBN only, so the design is all we do
