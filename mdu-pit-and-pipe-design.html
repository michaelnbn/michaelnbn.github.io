Pit and pipe design for multi dwelling unit developments

For buildings and sites with multiple dwellings in one lot, including apartment buildings and sites with shared land owned by a body corporate.

Reviewed 4 October 2026 against NBN's published guidance

What counts as an MDU

NBN defines a multi dwelling unit as a building containing multiple dwellings within a single lot. There are two main kinds.

Vertical MDU

A building such as an apartment block or office block, where the dwellings sit above and beside each other inside one structure.

Horizontal MDU (HMDU)

An MDU where the pit and pipe works sit on part of the site that will be owned by a body corporate. NBN publishes separate guidelines for HMDUs.

Conduit sizes

For a vertical MDU with up to 60 internal premises, NBN requires a P50 service drop conduit per premises.

For a vertical MDU with more than 60 internal premises, NBN requires a P100 service drop conduit.

What the design covers

  • Pit locations and conduit routes from the street network to the building or common property
  • Conduit sizes and lengths for each premises
  • Existing roads and telecom pits around the site, documented in the design
  • A design prepared for review through the nbn New Developments portal

What we need to start

  • Site address and state or territory
  • Number of dwellings and the stage of the development
  • CAD (DWG) and/or PDF plans showing the building, common property and surrounding roads
  • Your NBN application reference, if you have one
  • Dial Before You Dig or site survey results, if you have them
